## The change

No retrieval mechanics changed. Three sentences were added to the three
Jev prompts, so that Jev — not a file-extension list — decides that a
derived copy of text is a dead end:

- Scoring criteria (`_jev_select`, the 400-candidate pass), first "not
  relevant" clause: "… or is a derived copy of text that exists elsewhere
  in the repository in its original form — a translation or localisation
  catalog, a mirrored or generated file — which adds nothing the original
  does not".
- Chain instruction (`_jev_expand`, the full judgement): "A derived copy
  of text that exists elsewhere in the repository in its original form …
  adds nothing the original does not and is a dead end."
- Hit-triage instruction: "A translation or localisation catalog, or
  another derived copy of text that exists elsewhere in its original
  form, does not help." (The build-file sentence in the same instruction
  is kept.)

Reason: run b, Q5 and Q7 — `po4a/*.po` man-page translations seeded the
expansion (Q5: one, 490 k chars in; Q7: six, 724 k chars in, 230 k of the
252 k writer tokens) and the writer's output varied by 1.5 points on
identical evidence. An extension skip list (`.po`, `.pot`) was considered
and rejected: it names one project's convention, and the next repository
has `.json` locale files, vendored copies, generated headers.
