## Findings of the day

- Seed scores from the full model move 0.05–0.1 between runs. With the
  cut at 2.0 this flipped bodies in and out. With K=100 and the cut at
  1.9 the Q4 seeds were identical in four runs.
- Flash chain verdicts repeat exactly for the same input.
- The writer drops evidence that is in its context. It happens more on
  large contexts (cheap profile, 340–370 k chars) but also at 147 k
  (Q4 run 4). Not addressed.
- Stable flash misses: build and script files (`CMakeLists.txt`,
  `license-check.sh`, `tests/files/README`) are rejected.
- Signpost (`JEV_SIGNPOST=1`) earned at most 0.25 on Q4 for +20–30 %
  judge cost. Full-model retry (`JEV_RETRY_MODEL`) earned nothing on Q4
  and Q8 for +45–60 %. Both are in the working copy, off, not committed.
- Q4 on the other profiles today: cheap K=20 4.5; cheap K=100 5.25 and
  4.0; cheap K=100 cut 1.9 5.25 and 3.75; cheap K=200 4.25; coverage 5.75.
  The cheap 11-06 score of 6.5 was not reproduced; the cause is unknown.
