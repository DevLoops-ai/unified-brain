## Key correction — Q8 item (3)

The Q8 key said the "VLI width" survive-then-fail case (change a Record's
VLI encoding without changing its value, so the Index length no longer
matches Backward Size) is caught at the `index_list_size` comparison in
`lzma_index_hash_decode()`. It is not: it is caught earlier, by the VLI
decoder rejecting non-minimal encodings —
`src/liblzma/common/vli_decoder.c:43`,
`if (byte == 0x00 && *vli_pos > 1) return LZMA_DATA_ERROR;`. An answer
that says "the VLI decoder rejects a non-minimal encoding" is right and
scores the item; an answer that routes it to the size comparison does
not. The afternoon K=400 Q9 answer (below) had it right and was regraded
5.5 → 5.75 on its Q8 part under this correction. `evals/questions/xz.md`
carries the fix from this file on.

  skipped most of what they were shown and the skips were the right

  The two misses of the mechanism (Q5's `lzma_stream_header_decode`,
  open62541's `setOverflowInfoBits`) are both short functions the judge

  many lines of it is the thing to look at before touching a threshold.

  question. Seeds: 2 / 12 / 10 / 4 / 3 / 5 / 6 / 5 / 11 (f-pplx: 2 / 20
  / 22 / 8 / 4 / 7 / 6 / 10 / 15). On the broad questions the chains

  narrow ones there is no slack — Q5 with 3 seeds lost a full point to
  one judge "no". K=40 is the setting if that slack is wanted; this

- **Per-ancestor 15 k.** Context sizes stayed in f-pplx's range (1.84 M
  chars vs 1.95 M); the one probable cut is Q9's `hardware.c`.
- **Generic-mention fan-out** is now the visible waste: `common.c` →
