## The change

Three edits in `_jev_expand`, all from the misses listed at the end of
`qa-xz-2026-11-01.md`.

1. **Struct members as names.** `_name_pool` offered Jev only *defined*
   symbols found in a body. Q7's link to `io_open_src_real()` runs through
   `io_unlink(…, &pair->src_st)` — `src_st` is a member, not a symbol, so
   Jev was never offered it. Now `->word` / `.word` tokens (4+ characters,
   `_MEMBER_RE`) that are not defined symbols are added to the pool; Jev
   decides whether to follow them like any other name. Shaped on C; a
   comment in the code records that it is NOT yet measured on a dynamic
   language, where `.` is everywhere.
2. **Triage every place of a followed name.** A name with more than
   `JEV_EXPAND_MAX_HITS` (25) places was dropped whole — the count decided,
   not the judge. Now every place goes to the hit triage, 30 per Jev call.
   Reverse links (places that mention the reached file's *name*, which
   skip the triage since change 1) keep the cap, because without the
   triage they would be unbounded untriaged reads.
3. **Helpers of evidence.** A body Jev read and marked "continue" (a lead:
   useful for navigation, not evidence) stayed a one-line lead even when
   the body it was reached *from* became evidence. Pre-round log, Q2:
   `prepare_for_strict_sandbox()` read, "continue" twice, never shown to
   the writer. Now a "continue" body whose parent is evidence is attached
   as evidence at the end of the expansion, within the existing budgets.

Pre-round checks (14:22–14:29, with 1 and 2, before 3): Q7 5.5 / 6
(`io_open_src_real()` reached and used, first time), Q8 5 / 6, Q2 4 / 5
(the `prepare_for_strict_sandbox()` log above). Then edit 3, then the run.
